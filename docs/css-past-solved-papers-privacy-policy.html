Privacy Policy

CSS Past Papers - Solved Papers
Effective date: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CSS Past Papers - Solved Papers handles information when you use the application on Android, Windows, web or another supported platform.

The application is designed as an offline educational study tool. It does not require an account, and the current release does not sell personal information or include advertising or analytics SDKs.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The app may store the following locally so its features work between sessions:

  • Bookmarks for papers and questions
  • MCQ practice attempts, scores and progress
  • Theme, text-size and Home-screen display preferences
  • Admin settings, including the locally configured admin password
  • Paper records imported or published through the Admin section

This information is stored on the device using local application storage. It is not automatically sent to the developer.

2. Files You Choose to Import

If you use the Admin import feature, you may select a Word or PDF file through the operating system file picker, or paste extracted text. The selected content is used to create a local paper preview and, after administrator verification, a local paper record. The app does not upload the selected file to the developer in the current release.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

We do not intentionally request or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photographs, precise location, CNIC information, payment information or private messages. The app does not require user registration or an online account.

4. Network Access and Third Parties

The core reader, paper library, bookmarks and practice features are intended to work offline. The current release does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or external AI SDKs. The operating system, app store, hosting provider or future optional service may process technical information under its own privacy policy.

Future releases may add online services. If a feature sends information outside the device, this policy will be updated before or when that feature is introduced.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Data stored only on your device is not shared by the developer. You remain responsible for any files or information you voluntarily share through your device, operating system or third-party services.

6. Data Deletion

You can remove local app data by using available in-app controls, clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstalling the application. Imported papers, bookmarks, practice history and preferences stored only on your device cannot be deleted remotely by the developer.

7. Children's Privacy

This is a general educational examination-preparation application. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures available to the application platform to protect locally stored application data. No electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. The admin password is intended to control the local Admin interface and should not be reused for another service.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, its data practices or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made. The latest version will remain available at this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, technical concerns or requests relating to this application, contact the developer through the support contact published on the app's official store listing. Please include enough information to identify the request and do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

11. Independent Educational Resource

CSS Past Papers - Solved Papers is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or officially connected with the Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC), Government of Pakistan, or any other examination authority. Examination questions and AI-assisted solutions are provided for educational and examination-preparation purposes. Official examination information should be verified through the relevant official authority.
