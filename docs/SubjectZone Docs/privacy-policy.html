Google Play Data Safety Compliance

Privacy Policy — SubjectZone

Effective Date: October 31, 2026  |  App Version: 1.0.0  |  Package: com.subjectzone.app

🔒 At a Glance Privacy Commitment

SubjectZone has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The solved exercises are stored inside the app, your place in the library is kept only while the app is open, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal information.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SubjectZone ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information across its distributed editions: Android (Google Play Store), Windows, and Web.

SubjectZone provides solved textbook exercises for Classes IX–XII in a hand-built question-and-answer library. It is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any textbook board. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

SubjectZone does not collect personal information. In particular:

  • there is no registration, sign-in, account or user profile;
  • the App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location;
  • there are no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking services in the App;
  • the App sets no cookies and keeps no usage history on a server;
  • your reading preferences (typeface, text size, weight, spacing and colour) are saved only in the App’s local storage on your device and are never transmitted to us;
  • queries typed or spoken in the App are not uploaded to us or stored in a history.

The App does not need any of this information to work: the textbook content and solutions are bundled with the App, and your current place in the library exists only in memory while the App is open.

3. Device Permissions and Their Purpose

3.1 Microphone (Voice Search)

Microphone access is requested only when you tap the microphone button and choose to use voice navigation (for example, saying “Class IX”, a subject name, or “Next”). Audio is handled by the speech-recognition service built into your browser or operating system, as explained in Section 4. SubjectZone does not record, store, or transmit your audio, and declining the permission leaves every feature available by touch or keyboard.

3.2 Internet Access

  • Installed Android and Windows builds: content is read from files bundled inside the App, so displaying a solution requires no network request. Internet access is used only when you choose to open an external link such as an email address or the project’s GitHub page.
  • Web edition: your browser downloads the App’s own static files from its hosting provider, as described in Section 5.

The App requests no access to storage, contacts, camera, location, calendar, SMS, or phone state.

4. Voice Features

4.1 Voice Search

Voice search uses the speech-recognition capability of your browser or operating system. Depending on your device and its settings, that service may send microphone audio to its provider — for example Google or Microsoft — to convert it into text. That processing is governed by the provider’s own privacy policy. SubjectZone receives only the recognised text, uses it to open the requested class, subject, or unit, and does not retain it.

4.2 Read-Aloud

Read-aloud passes the answer text to the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. SubjectZone does not collect or transmit that text. Whether a voice works locally or online is decided by your operating system and is covered by its privacy settings.

You can stop a voice session at any time by closing the microphone or stopping playback. No audio recording is ever saved by the App.

5. Network Connections

  • The installed Windows and Android builds display content without contacting any server.
  • The web edition downloads the same static files from its hosting provider. Like any web server, the host may record standard request information such as an IP address, browser type, and the time of the request. We do not receive, combine, or analyse that information.
  • The App loads no third-party fonts, analytics scripts, advertisements, or content networks. Fonts and icons are included with the App.
  • Links to email and GitHub open only when you choose them, in your default browser or mail app.

6. Third-Party Services

SubjectZone does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social SDK. The only external processing that can occur is the speech-recognition and text-to-speech functionality provided by your device’s operating system or browser, described in Section 4, and the app distribution platforms that deliver the App (Google Play, Microsoft Store, or your web browser). Those platforms process install and update data under their own privacy policies.

7. Children’s Privacy (COPPA and GDPR-K)

SubjectZone is an educational study aid intended for secondary and higher-secondary students, their teachers, and parents. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country).

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has sent personal details to us by email, they can contact us and the message will be deleted.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

There is no account and no server-side profile, so there is no stored personal data to retain or delete. App data on your device consists only of the installed application files, ordinary cache, and your saved reading preferences; uninstalling the App, clearing its storage in your device settings, or choosing Reset to defaults in the reading settings removes that information. Step-by-step instructions are available on our Account and Data Deletion page.

If you have sent us an email, that message remains in the support mailbox only until the request has been handled, and you may ask for it to be deleted at any time.

9. Security

SubjectZone stores its content locally and does not operate a database of user information, which limits the data that could be exposed. Any web-edition files are delivered over HTTPS by the hosting provider. Because no account exists, there are no passwords or sign-in credentials for us to protect.

10. Changes to This Policy

If the App’s data practices change — for example, if an online feature is introduced — this policy will be updated before that feature is released, with a new effective date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the App after an update means you accept the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page occasionally.
