Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CSS MPT Practice Question Bank handles information when you use the app. The app is designed as an offline study and practice tool for CSS MPT and full-paper preparation.

The app does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not send your practice activity to our servers.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or payment details.

The app may store the following information locally on your device so the practice experience works correctly:

  • Practice progress and attempt history
  • Bookmarks and review status
  • Quiz preferences and selected reading settings
  • Theme, typography, and dashboard display preferences

How Information Is Used

Locally stored information is used only to provide app features, including progress tracking, bookmarked questions, review screens, saved preferences, and personalized display settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking tools.

Internet Access

The app is intended to work offline. Development or platform builds may include standard framework permissions for debugging or platform operation, but the app itself does not transmit your study data to an external server.

Data Storage and Deletion

Practice data and preferences are stored on your device. You can clear this data by using the app's Reset Progress option in Settings or by uninstalling the app from your device.

Children's Privacy

The app is an educational study tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features or legal requirements change. Updates will be reflected by changing the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the app developer through the support contact listed on the app's Google Play Store page.
