Privacy Policy — USE Advance Picture Dictionary

Effective Date: October 26, 2026  |  Package: com.useadvance.use_advance_picture_dictionary_app

🔒 At a Glance Privacy Commitment

USE Advance Picture Dictionary is designed with offline-first educational accessibility in mind. Your dictionary searches, learning history, and scanned images remain strictly on your device. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how USE Advance Picture Dictionary ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") handles your data across all distributed platforms, including Android (Google Play Store), Windows, and Web editions.

By using the Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Data Collection and Usage

2.1 On-Device Offline Operation

The Application contains an extensive pre-packaged visual vocabulary database with 15,916+ illustrated flashcards, definitions, grammar categories, and translations in Sindhi and Urdu. All core search, browsing, bookmarks, and vocabulary learning features function completely offline on your device without transmitting your search queries to any remote servers.

2.2 Text-to-Speech (Pronunciation)

The Application includes audio pronunciation of English vocabulary terms using your device's built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine. Audio playback is generated locally on your device. No voice data or recordings are captured, stored, or transmitted over the internet.

2.3 Image Import and Optical Character Recognition (OCR)

The Application allows users to import vocabulary card images or split grids to import custom visual cards:

  • Photo / File Picker: When you select an image file to import cards, access is strictly limited to the specific file you choose.
  • Local Processing: OCR text recognition and card image splitting are processed entirely locally on your device. Selected images and scanned text are never uploaded to any external server.

2.4 Optional Cloud Sync & Authentication

The Application offers optional multi-device synchronization via Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication:

  • Anonymous Authentication: To allow seamless read-only synchronization of public updates, the app may use anonymous Firebase authentication, which generates a random, non-identifying technical identifier (UID).
  • User Preferences & Bookmarks: When cloud sync is explicitly enabled by the user, saved bookmarks or custom learning progress are stored securely in Cloud Firestore tied only to your anonymous or administrator account.
  • No Tracking: Firebase services are used solely for backend database synchronization and authentication, not for ad profiling or behavioral tracking across apps.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

  • INTERNET (android.permission.INTERNET): Required for optional Firebase database synchronization, loading remote updates, and inter-app URL scheme communication.
  • Storage / Media Access: Only accessed via system-managed file pickers when you explicitly choose to import card pictures or export dictionary backups.

4. Third-Party Services

The Application utilizes trusted services provided by Google LLC:

  • Google Play Services: Core Android application runtime and updates (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore: Cloud synchronization and data management (Firebase Privacy Information).

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

USE Advance Picture Dictionary is a general-audience educational vocabulary learning application suitable for learners of all ages, including students and children. The Application does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at useadvancedictionary@gmail.com, and we will promptly delete such information.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

All local data (bookmarks, search history, custom cards) is stored on your device and can be cleared at any time by selecting "Clear Data / Clear Cache" in your device's App Settings or by uninstalling the Application.

For any data synced with our optional cloud services, you have the right to request permanent deletion at any time. Detailed instructions are available on our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Page.

7. Security

We value your trust. Any network transmissions for optional cloud sync use industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption to prevent unauthorized interception. Local data is isolated within the Application's private sandboxed sandbox storage directory.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. You are advised to review this page occasionally for any changes.
