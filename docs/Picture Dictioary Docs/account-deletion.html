Account and Data Deletion — USE Advance Picture Dictionary

Application: USE Advance Picture Dictionary  |  Package: com.useadvance.use_advance_picture_dictionary_app

In accordance with Google Play Store Developer Policies and global privacy regulations, users of USE Advance Picture Dictionary have full control over their personal data. This page outlines the simple procedure to request permanent deletion of your account and any associated cloud data stored by the developer.

1. How to Submit a Deletion Request

Submit Your Request via Email

1
Compose an email to our support team at useadvancedictionary@gmail.com.
2
Set the subject line to: Account and Data Deletion Request - USE Advance Picture Dictionary.
3
Provide identifying information so we can locate your record:
  • Your registered email address (if you signed in via Firebase email/password).
  • Or mention your Anonymous Firebase User Identifier (UID) if available under Settings.
  • Specify whether you want deletion of your authentication account, synchronized bookmarks/progress, or all records.

2. Data Types and Deletion Breakdown

Data Category What Happens Upon Request Retention / Deletion Policy
Account Credentials (Firebase Auth Email / Password / UID) Permanently deleted from Firebase Authentication servers. Deleted within 30 days of verified request.
Cloud Synced Data (Bookmarks, Favorites, Custom Vocabulary Entries in Firestore) Permanently purged from Cloud Firestore database collections. Deleted within 30 days of verified request.
Local On-Device Data (Local SQLite database, search history, cached card images) Managed directly on your device. Can be deleted instantly without waiting (see Section 4). Instant on-device user removal.
Diagnostic / Security Logs Standard anonymous network logs that do not identify individuals. Automatically discarded per Google Cloud standard rotation (up to 30 days).

3. Request Processing Timeline

Upon receiving your email request:

  • We will acknowledge receipt of your request within 48 hours.
  • We may ask for verification to ensure the request is from the legitimate account owner.
  • All associated server-side data will be permanently wiped within 30 days, and a final confirmation email will be sent to you.

4. How to Delete Local Data On Your Device Immediately

Because the core dictionary is offline-first, you can instantly remove all locally stored vocabulary history, card imports, and cached data directly from your device at any time:

  1. Open your Android device Settings.
  2. Navigate to Apps or Application Manager → USE Advance Picture Dictionary.
  3. Tap Storage & Cache.
  4. Select Clear Storage (or Clear Data) and Clear Cache.
  5. Alternatively, uninstalling the Application removes all local application sandbox storage immediately.
⚠️ Warning: Account and cloud deletion is irreversible. Once deleted, any custom vocabulary or synced bookmarks cannot be recovered.
